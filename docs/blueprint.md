# **App Name**: Pawsitive Cart

## Core Features:

- Product Browsing: Browse and search for products by category, pet type, or keyword.
- Product Details: View product details, including images, descriptions, reviews, and pricing.
- Shopping Cart: Add items to a virtual shopping cart.
- AI Smart Cart: A 'smart' shopping cart, where AI suggests extra products, acting as a tool to make sure you haven't forgotten anything that is appropriate for the items already in the cart.
- Checkout Process: Proceed to checkout and enter shipping information.
- Customer Accounts: Securely store the customer's shipping and credit card information.
- Order Tracking: Track order status and view order history.

## Style Guidelines:

- Primary color: Warm, earthy brown (#A0522D) to evoke feelings of nature, safety, and comfort, suitable for pet-related products.
- Background color: Soft, desaturated beige (#F5F5DC), providing a neutral and calming backdrop to allow the products to stand out.
- Accent color: Muted orange (#E07A5F) to draw attention to key actions without being overwhelming, while complementing the analogous brown tones.
- Friendly, rounded sans-serif fonts to convey warmth and approachability.
- Custom icons related to pet care, using a consistent, playful style.
- Clean and spacious layout with clear product categorization and intuitive navigation.
- Subtle transitions and animations to enhance user engagement, such as a 'wagging tail' effect for 'add to cart'.
